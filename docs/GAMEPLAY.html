FloraForge · Design Notes

From sandbox
to game.

FloraForge already contains a real evolutionary simulation — but the player is a pure spectator. These notes map the option space for player agency, progression, and goals, and recommend a path. Nothing here is committed yet.

brainstorm not committed breeder-spine recommended

00 · Baseline

Where we are today

The sim is the crown jewel; almost nothing lets the player touch it. The continuous question driving every choice below: what is the player's role? Pick that, and the tech tree and win conditions fall out of it.

What already exists

  • 8 heritable genes per plant (wet_pref, alt_pref, stress_width, capture, fecundity, seed_mass, dispersal, timing) with inheritance + mutation.
  • Phenotype → fitness mapping: abiotic fitness, competition room, size/lifespan scaling, establishment chance, leaf tint.
  • Lifecycle: seedling → young → mature → dead snag → despawn.
  • Spread / reproduction tick: mature plants emit seeds by fecundity + dispersal; seedlings establish by establishment chance.
  • Census, Population Lens, Evolution overlays: live gene/phenotype stats, population by stage, time-series graph.
  • Houses: procedurally placed, block plant spread; static decoration, no inhabitants.
Missing entirely: player resources, inventory, score, goals, currency, and win conditions. The player currently only watches.

01 · Identity

The four candidate player roles

Recommended spine: A (Breeder), with B and C as supporting loops. It is the only direction that is uniquely ours — nobody else has this genetics engine, and the name literally is FloraForge. D (settlement survival) is the most conventionally "gamey" but competes with Banished / Timberborn and would bury our best system under city-management busywork.

02 · Progression shape

The biology tree — our "tech tree"

A literal tech tree feels wrong for a plant game. Two better-fitting shapes:

2aTrait Unlock Tree — authored

Unlock capabilities to manipulate genes, not new tech. Branches map to the 8 gene loci:

Dispersaldispersal · seed_mass
Observe dispersal → favor long-dispersers when culling → splice the dispersal gene directly.
Hardinesswet_pref · alt_pref · stress_width
Push a species into hostile biomes — desert, alpine, underwater.
Vigorfecundity · capture · timing
Faster reproduction, denser canopies.
MorphologyPlant Editor hook
Unlock leaf types, crown shapes, and sizes as "discovered mutations" rather than free sliders.

Progress currency = observation: earn points by watching real evolutionary events — a species colonizing a new biome, an allele crossing 50% frequency, first death, a successful long-distance dispersal. The census + Lens already compute what's needed to detect these.

2bPhylogenetic / Speciation Tree — emergent

The tree is generated by play: when a population's gene distribution drifts far enough from its parent, it forks into a new branch you name and add to the Herbarium. Progress is literally a growing tree of life you bred — the Herbarium becomes a trophy case of species you caused to exist. Most novel, most on-theme.

Plan: ship 2a first (buttons to push), grow toward 2b (the long-term payoff).

03 · Agency

The moment-to-moment verbs

Currently zero. Cheapest high-impact additions, in priority order:

  1. Plant / seed a species at the cursor. Raycast to terrain — we already raycast for map-click teleport and house placement. Instant "I did that."
  2. Cull. Remove a plant or clear a radius. Pairs with breeding: cull the unfit, let the fit spread → the player becomes natural selection.
  3. Cross-pollinate / tag-as-parent. Pick two plants, force their genes into the next local spread round. The spread tick already does inheritance + mutation, so this only overrides parent selection.
  4. Irrigate / terraform a tile. Locally nudge the moisture/altitude field so a species can establish where it couldn't. Phenotype eval already keys off local wet/altitude.

Even just #1 + #2 turns it from screensaver into game.

04 · Economy

Progression & resources

Keep it thematic — avoid gold / XP. Two candidate economies:

Biomass / Biodiversity

Passive, observational. Derived from the existing census — total living biomass + a Shannon diversity index across species. Spend on tree unlocks. Rewards a healthy ecosystem, not a monoculture.

Genome / Seed bank

Active, collectible. Sample plants to bank their genomes — the player's inventory. Replant anywhere, breed from them, or "complete" a species. The save already stores genes per plant, so a seed bank is a small addition.

05 · Purpose

Win & goal conditions

Not a single binary win — stacked objectives, so there is always a next thing.

Short · session
Expeditions. "Establish a self-sustaining population in the desert," "breed a tree that survives above the snow line," "get species X to 1,000 individuals." All checkable from census + phenotype data we already compute.
Medium
Herbarium completion. Discover / breed all base species into stable wild populations, then the emergent variants (2b).
Long · sandbox
Green the world — bring living flora to N% of land across all biomes (Terraformer victory). Or Tree of Life — speciate K distinct lineages (Breeder victory). Reuse the loading screen's 256×256 world map widget as a live victory tracker.

06 · Delivery

Recommended rollout

Three increments, each playable on its own:

01Agency MVP

Plant + cull at cursor, a biomass score, and 3 expedition goals. Turns spectator → player. Small.

02Breeder loop

Cross-pollinate / parent-select + a seed bank + Trait Unlock Tree (2a). Adds depth + progression.

03Speciation & victory

Emergent phylogenetic tree (2b) + the two long-term win conditions. Adds the endgame and the "wow."
